How to use

  1. Categories (☰) se category chuno, uska safha khul jata hai.
  2. Upar Back aur Next se safhon ke beech jao. Aakhri safhe par Next naya safha bana deta hai.
  3. Example lines ko apni raqam se badal do, ya nayi lines likho.
  4. # se heading likho, khali line se naya hissa shuru hota hai.
  5. total us hisse ki upar wali lines ka jod hai.
  6. ~ wali line total mein nahi ginti, limit = 25k se hadd lagti hai.
  7. Jawab par tap karo to copy hota hai. Categories ke andar PDF button se hisaab save karo.

Hisaab

Calculator ke button nahi, seedha apni zubaan mein likho. Har line ka jawab aur chalta hua total saath saath aata hai.

Naam do aur dobara use karo: tankhwah = 85k. Khali line ke baad naya hissa shuru hota hai. total us hisse ki upar wali lines jod deta hai, pichla upar wali line ka jawab hai. Agli line par tap karo to wahan seedha likh sakte ho. Jawab par tap karo to copy ho jata hai.

Naye features: ~ bachat = a - b (sirf jawab dikhao, total mein na gino), limit = 25k (hisse ki hadd, zyada hone par laal), 2 tola to gram 5 seer to kg 1 maund to kg, 15 percent of 8000. Graph mein Bars/Donut badal sakte ho, aur do ya zyada safhon ka muqabla graph ke neeche aata hai.
