How to use

  1. Categories tab se category chuno, uska safha khul jata hai.
  2. Upar ← aur → se safhon ke beech jao. Aakhri safhe par → naya safha bana deta hai.
  3. Line par tap karke likho, jaise 5000 ka 15%. Jawab saath hi dikhta hai.
  4. # se heading likho, khali line se naya hissa shuru hota hai. total us hisse ki upar wali lines ka jod hai.
  5. ~ wali line total mein nahi ginti. limit = 25k se hisse ki hadd lagti hai.
  6. Keyboard ke upar ↵ nayi line banata hai aur ⌫ line hatata hai.
